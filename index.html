Some stories refuse to be forgotten.

Welcome to my field journal. This archive documents investigations into unexplained occurrences, forgotten histories and places that have long been abandoned to time.

What follows is an account of my visits to the Prabhakar Haveli, and the strange events that took place within its walls. Every entry has been documented as accurately as possible.

THE FEATURED INVESTIGATION CASE FILE NO. 001

MAHARASHTRA, INDIA

The Prabhakar Haveli

Once home to the wealthy Prabhakar family, the Haveli has been the subject of rumours and unsettling local accounts for decades. At the centre of these stories is Kamla, a woman whose fate remains unknown.

What began as an attempt to document a local legend would soon become a series of investigations that raised more questions than answers.
