FIELD REPORT NO. 002 · FOLLOW-UP INVESTIGATION

The Second Investigation

LOCATION: MAHARASHTRA, INDIA · INVESTIGATION 02

It has been a little over a week since our initial visit to the Prabhakar Haveli, and I think it is time for an update. After reviewing the footage and recordings from our previous investigation, we decided that another visit was necessary. There were still a lot of questions that I want to find the answers to, and we felt that a second investigation might be what can help.

Before I explain our visit, I would like to address a few of the comments left on our previous blog. While I understand that not everyone believes in the paranormal, I find it rather sad that some people have not only decided to dismiss our findings, but have also left a few comments disrespecting the history of the Haveli. They might not believe in the supernatural, but disrespecting the dead is not okay. I would also like to clarify that our intention was never to fabricate events for engagement, but rather to bring attention to the many legends that make up our history. We simply reported our experiences.

Now, back to the visit. We have decided to go back to the Haveli for another visit, but only this time we kept the team smaller. Only five of us made the trip, including myself. We wanted to reduce chances of disruptions due to the numbers and conduct a more thorough investigation of the property, particularly the first floor, where most of the occurrences had been reported during our previous visit.

We tried to keep everything similar to the previous visit, down to the time we arrived. The property seemed to be unchanged, including the slightly ajar door. Like last time, there was no evidence to suggest that anyone had been there.

We began by setting up our equipment and for the first half hour everything seemed to be ordinary. It was also quieter than our previous visit due to the lack of people. There were no footsteps, creaking sounds or anything of that sort. Yet.

It was only when we made our way upstairs that things began to change. One of the team members, who had been inspecting a room at the end of the corridor, called out to us as he claimed to have heard anklet sounds coming from a room down the corridor. None of the other team members heard that, though.

When we entered the room, there was nothing unusual to be found. The windows were shut, the furniture was covered in dust, and there was nothing to indicate that anyone had been inside. We searched all the rooms before and found nothing, so we decided to continue with the investigation.

However, as we were making our way back towards the staircase, the crew member told us he could hear the anklet sounds again. But he didn't need to tell us, because we could all hear it now. It was coming from downstairs.

We stood there for a few moments, listening and making sure. It was faint, the kind of sounds you can hear only if you strain your ears, but it was there. There was no mistaking the sound we heard. It sounded like a woman walking through the ground floor of the house. Right as we decided to go check it out, it stopped.

We returned to the ground floor only to find that a portrait that was supposed to be hanging on the wall was no longer there. It had been placed on the small table near the entrance, facing towards the staircase.

It was a portrait of Kamla.

I initially thought that perhaps one of the team members had moved it, but the others have denied doing so. We decided to inspect the photograph more closely. While examining it, we noticed that there was a crack running through the glass, going through Kamla's face exactly, obscuring her face. I don't usually read into such things, and it is entirely possible that the portrait fell sometime between our last visit and this one, but the portrait moving and the positioning of the crack were too perfect considering the circumstances.

Shortly after this, we decided to pack up. As the last of our crew left the house, the door slammed shut, and we all froze. From inside the house, there were faint noises of the anklets and the same sound I heard while leaving the house on our first visit. A woman crying. The crew could only hear the anklets but not the crying. For some reason I'm the only one who could hear her grief. We left the property shortly afterwards.

However, ever since returning, I have gone through all the footage several times. Unfortunately, the audio equipment did not capture much of what we heard. Though, there is one particular recording that I have been unable to explain. At approximately the same time that we heard the anklets, the audio picks up what sounds like someone whispering. I have listened to it repeatedly, but I cannot make out what is being said.

Again, I am aware that some of you will no doubt find an explanation for what happened. And some of you might call us insane. And that is perfectly fine. However, I would appreciate it if people stopped treating the story and history of Kamla as a joke. We are dealing with an actual location that has real history with actual people involved. They are not characters in some cheap horror novel written for your entertainment.

There is clearly something about this house that we have yet to understand. And clearly I am the only one who can hear Kamla and her suffering. I will go back and I will bring back satisfactory results.

Until next time.

END OF FIELD REPORT NO. 002
