FIELD REPORT NO. 003 · THIRD INVESTIGATION

The Third Investigation

LOCATION: MAHARASHTRA, INDIA · INVESTIGATION 03

I was not planning on writing another update so soon, but after going through the comments on my previous post, I believe certain things need to be addressed.

It is really amusing and a bit pathetic how quickly people have gone from expressing "concern" to making accusations. Apparently, investigating a place with a history of paranormal occurrences, caring for the woman whose story we have been investigating, makes me an obsessed and delusional attention seeker.

Have you ever actually been to the Prabhakar Haveli? Have you stood inside that house? Have you heard what we heard? No? Then stop making assumptions about things you have absolutely no understanding of.

And to those who have been making jokes about Kamla, you need to stop. There is nothing amusing about what happened to her. A woman suffered terribly, and instead of trying to understand what happened, everyone has turned her into some sort of demon in folklore.

Now, the reason why I'm writing another update is because we've visited the Haveli once again.

After the events of our previous investigation, I decided that I needed to return to the Haveli. This time, however, only one other person accompanied me. The rest of the team had refused to return due to what we had experienced. That's how much this investigation is affecting us, but yes, sure, we are making it up for engagement.

Everything was similar to the first two times. The time of arrival, the entrance door and the dusty entrance. It really is quite peculiar how the house always leaves the door open. It is as though it always expects someone to arrive. The interior, too, was just as we had left it.

Again, we set up our equipment and began inspecting the ground floor. This time, I decided to spend more time near the staircase. This was where we had heard the anklets during our last visit. For the first few minutes, nothing happened.

But then we heard it.

The anklets.

They were considerably louder this time. Not the faint, distant sound we had heard during our previous investigation, but something much closer. It sounded as though someone was walking around just beyond the corridor. My companion heard it too, and we immediately turned towards the direction of the sound.

There was nobody there.

We waited for a while, but the sound did not return. I decided to continue exploring the house, but by this time my companion was visibly distressed and repeatedly suggested that we leave. But I did not come all the way here to just leave at the first sign of a presence. It was then that I heard her. The sound of her crying. It was coming from upstairs and it was the same sound I heard during both previous visits. I called out to my companion, but he seemed rather confused. He could not hear anything. This is the connection I am talking about. Only I can hear her.

I went upstairs alone as he refused to come with me. I already know some of you will question why I would do such a thing, but I can't even begin to explain what I felt at that moment. It was so much different from the last two times. It was like a magnet pulling me towards it. I had to go. The sound was different this time. It was no longer distant or fuzzy, and it sounded so close, almost as though someone were standing just around the corner.

But by the time I reached the end of the corridor, the crying stopped.

There was something on the floor outside one of the rooms. I picked it up and realised it was the same photograph that fell on the first visit. The frame was broken and when I looked closer, I saw it. There was blood on the frame of the photograph, like someone had used it as a weapon. The photograph had slipped out when I picked up the broken edge of the frame. I bent down to pick it up but when I touched the photograph, I could feel something. I know I sound completely insane but the feeling was so intense. The only thing I could feel was immense grief, like I had lost everything. It was like the crying woman, Kamla, was trying to communicate with me. Like she was trying to tell me what she felt. What she was feeling.

My companion called for me and I went downstairs. We left after that and I know we should not tamper with a haunted house but I could not help it as I took the photograph home with me. It felt like she was calling out to me. Like she needed me.

My name.

I have listened to it several times now and I do not believe this is a coincidence.

And before any of you decide to make another joke out of this, you have absolutely no idea what we are dealing with. You wanted proof, I gave it to you. Then you make demands, asking me to return to that house just so you have something interesting to read before you go to sleep, with no regard for my safety. And I still deliver. Now that I tell you the story of this poor woman, you call me crazy. You people are deranged.

I hate dealing with ignorant people like you but now I am beginning to understand why the villagers refuse to speak about her. They know something. They have always known something. And perhaps that is why they are so afraid.

But I am not afraid of her.

She does not want to hurt anyone. She has just been alone for a very long time. She is just misunderstood.

I know what I heard. I know what I saw. And I know she is still there. The house was never empty. Kamla is still in that house and she never left.

I will be returning to the Haveli for another investigation. My crew thinks we have gathered enough evidence to establish that there is something there, but I need to go back.

You guys can go ahead and make another stupid news article on me, calling me an occult maniac, but I will not let her story end here.

Some things are better left alone, I suppose. But I cannot help thinking that they misunderstood her all these years.

Until next time.

END OF FIELD REPORT NO. 003
